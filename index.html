Dear Socheat,

I don't really know where to begin. Also, it's my first time writing a letter to someone, so I'm a bit nervous too.

When we first met in a game, I never expected that the person I met there would one day become someone this important to me. It's like listening to a song for the first time without knowing that it will become your favorite.

I'm so lucky that you still talk to me.

And I love that you don't seem to fake who you are. I love the way you treat me. I love all those little things about you that I probably couldn't even list if I tried. My words might eventually run out, but somehow, your qualities wouldn't.

You made me realize that even from far away, someone can become incredibly close to my heart. There's a whole distance between us, and sometimes I hate that distance more than I can explain. I wish I could be there when you're sad. I wish I could sit beside you when you feel lonely. I wish I could comfort you properly instead of being someone behind a screen, trying to find the right words.

Sometimes I wish I could simply be there.

Not because I think I can fix everything, but because when something hurts you, a part of me wishes I could take even a little bit of that pain away.

I've never met you in real life, but what I feel for you is real to me. I've never felt this much affection for someone before.

You brought a kind of happiness into my life that I didn't know I was missing. Now I have someone I can tell things to, someone who listens to my boring stories and endless yapping, someone I can talk to without constantly worrying about being judged. Somehow, life feels a little happier knowing that you exist somewhere in this world.

If life gives us the chance to meet someday, I want to make that day one you'll remember for the rest of your life.

Maybe we'd walk somewhere together. Maybe we'd sit on a beach at night and talk until we had nothing left to say. Maybe we'd go somewhere neither of us has ever been before. Maybe you'd decide what we should do, and I'd simply follow along.

Honestly, one day probably wouldn't be enough.

But I'd try my best to make every moment count.

And maybe, if we're lucky, that first meeting wouldn't be the end of a dream. Maybe it would be the beginning of something much bigger.

I know that's a lot to dream about.

But you are someone worth dreaming about.

And if you ever have a bad day, if you're lonely, or if you feel like nobody understands you, I hope you remember that I might not always be good at finding the perfect words to cheer you up. Sometimes I'll probably say something stupid. Sometimes I won't know what to say at all.

But I will always wish for you to be happy.

And I want you to know that you have me.

Whenever you need someone, whenever you want to talk, whenever you want to tell someone about your day, or whenever you just want to yap about something completely random, I'll be here.

No matter how far away I am.

Maybe I can't give you everything you dream of. Maybe I can't promise that life will always be easy. But if you ever choose to trust me, I'll spend every day trying to give you everything I possibly can.

You became important to me without me even realizing it.

And now, somehow, you've become someone I don't want to lose.

I love you, Socheat.

I love you because somewhere between all those ordinary conversations, silly moments, happy days, and difficult ones, my heart simply chose you.

So many things are still untold in my mind. I hope, someday, I get the chance to tell you all of this while sitting beside you instead of writing it from another country.

Until then...

And whenever the distance feels too big, remember that somewhere far away, there's someone who cares about you more than his words can properly explain.

Always,
